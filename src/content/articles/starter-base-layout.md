## Overview

Moving from a temporary shelter to a permanent base is one of the most satisfying transitions in survival Minecraft. This guide covers the layout principles I used when establishing my riverside compound.

## What I built / learned

A good starter base needs three things: **efficient storage access**, **room to grow**, and **clear paths** between functional areas.

My layout uses a central hallway with rooms branching off like an inventory grid — storage on the left, crafting and furnaces on the right, bedroom and map room at the back.

## Key decisions

- **Underground vs. surface:** I chose a hillside embed with a glass front facing the river.
- **Material palette:** Oak, cobblestone, and glass — easy to gather, easy to expand.
- **Ceiling height:** Three blocks in main rooms, two in utility corridors.

## Problems and solutions

The original storage room was too small. Instead of rebuilding, I dug an adjacent chamber and connected them with a 2-block-wide doorway — a lesson in planning expansion corridors early.
