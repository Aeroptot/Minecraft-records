## Overview

The original nether portal dumped me into a lava lake. After fire-resistance potions and a lot of cobblestone, I secured the area and started building a proper hub.

## What I built

A central 9×9 hub room with tunnels extending in four directions:

| Direction | Color | Destination |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| North | Red concrete | Stronghold approach |
| South | Yellow concrete | Mesa biome |
| East | Green concrete | Main base |
| West | Blue concrete | Village trading hall |

## Screenshots

Each tunnel is 3 blocks wide and 4 blocks tall — enough for minecarts and safe sprinting.

## Key decisions

- **One-block soul sand strips** in the floor for speed boost paths.
- **Ceiling at Y=128** in the nether to avoid ghast fireballs.
- **Compass room** at the hub center with maps of each destination.

## Problems and solutions

Piglin aggro in the hub was constant until I enclosed all gold blocks behind glass. Lesson learned: don't store gold ingots in open chests near piglin territory.

## Next steps

Extend the green tunnel to the iron farm area and add a fast-travel rail system.
