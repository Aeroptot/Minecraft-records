## Overview

Every world starts somewhere. Mine started on a gentle plains hillside with a river to the east and a dark oak forest to the north. The first priority was simple: don't die.

## What happened

I punched trees, crafted basic tools, and dug a 3×3×2 hole into the hillside before sunset. A single door, a crafting table, and a furnace — that was the entire base for the first three in-game days.

## Screenshots

The shelter was humble, but it had a view of the river at dawn. That view convinced me to stay in this area rather than wander further.

## Key decisions

- **Stay near spawn** for the first week rather than exploring far.
- **Mark the coordinates** of promising cave entrances with torches and cobble pillars.
- **Don't craft diamond tools yet** — iron was two caves away.

## Next steps

Gather food, find a village, and scout for a permanent base location along the river.
