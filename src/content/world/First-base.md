## Overview

A statis base is essential in early game period, where everything is scarce. Finding and choosing a village as base, mining, building simple machines, and upgrade armors are crucial steps in the early days.

## What I built

As seen in the overview of the base. I have the following sites:

1. An iron farm.
2. A villager breeding area where there are both beds and an area of arable land.
3. A furnace group powered by carpet duplication.
4. A simple trading hall to trade rare enchanted books.
5. Area of birch trees, area of carrots, area of bamboo, and area of sugarcane.
6. A simple cobblestone machine
7. A simple rail machine
8. Area for sheep, area for cows, and area of wheat.

## Key decisions

A village provides two important things: bed and villager. Bed, in the first day, is hard to find if you are unlucky to find sheep. Villager, is important because valuable enchanted books can only be acquired through trading. 

An iron farm, I believe, is a machine everyone must build, no matter if you are a player like me. A good supply of iron gives you more freedom. You can have a full iron armor without going underground, and have unlimited hoppers to build redstone machines.

More information about how to begin the world can be seen in my articles, where I will explain everything you need to know in the early days if you are struggling with survival.

I plan to build a wither skeleton farm in order to obtain beacons to speed up my efficiency at building more machines and upgrading the base.
