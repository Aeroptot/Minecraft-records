## Overview

After two weeks of nomadic mining, I committed to a location: a flat stretch of plains on a river bend, with clay deposits nearby and a cave system directly beneath.

## What I built

The compound started as a single L-shaped building and grew into a small campus:

- **Main hall** with storage, crafting, and map wall
- **Crop terraces** descending toward the river
- **Animal pen** with automatic chicken feeder
- **Nether portal** in a stone enclosure behind the main building

## Screenshots

The glass-front design gives a panoramic view of the river at sunrise — the main reason I chose this spot.

## Key decisions

- Built the storage room **first**, even before walls were finished.
- Left a 5-block gap between buildings for future expansion hallways.
- Used lanterns instead of torches for interior lighting.

## Problems and solutions

River flooding during rain was an unexpected issue. Raised the foundation by one block and added a retaining wall along the bank.

## Next steps

Connect to the nearest village with a nether tunnel and start planning the iron farm.
